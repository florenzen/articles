## In short

This website sets no cookies, uses no analytics or tracking tools and loads no content from other servers (no external fonts, scripts or embedded media). I do not collect or store any data about visitors myself.

## Hosting on GitHub Pages

The website is served by GitHub Pages, a service of GitHub Inc., 88 Colin P. Kelly Jr. Street, San Francisco, CA 94107, USA. When you open a page, GitHub processes technically necessary data, in particular your IP address, the date and time of the request, the requested address and browser information (user agent). GitHub uses this data to deliver the pages and to keep the service secure, and may log it for that purpose. I have no access to these logs.

The legal basis is Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR; my legitimate interest is serving the website securely and reliably. Data may be transferred to the USA in the process. GitHub is certified under the EU-US Data Privacy Framework. For details, see the [GitHub Privacy Statement](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement).

The same applies when a feed reader fetches the RSS/Atom feed.

## Local storage of the colour scheme choice

If you choose a light or dark colour scheme with the switch in the header, the choice is stored in your browser's local storage (`localStorage`) so that it is kept for your next visit. The value never leaves your browser and is not sent to me or anyone else. This storage is strictly necessary for a function you explicitly asked for (Section 25(2) no. 2 of the German TDDDG). Choosing "System" again, or deleting the site data in your browser, removes the entry.

## Links to other websites

Articles and the footer contain links to other websites, such as Mastodon or GitHub. Data is only sent to those providers once you follow a link; their privacy policies then apply.

## Your rights

Under the GDPR you have the right of access (Art. 15), rectification (Art. 16), erasure (Art. 17), restriction of processing (Art. 18), data portability (Art. 20) and to object to processing (Art. 21). As I do not store any data about you myself, these rights mainly concern the processing by GitHub; feel free to contact me or GitHub directly.

You also have the right to lodge a complaint with a data protection supervisory authority (Art. 77 GDPR).
